CHIM OStim Bridge - changelog
=============================

1.2.0
- New: thralls. A Vampire Lord - an AI agent, or the player once they are one - bites
  someone who is not a Vampire Lord (ordinary vampires too) and binds their will:
  the thrall obeys their master and the player in everything, scenes included, is
  never hostile and (MCM) fights on the player's side. AI actions Enthrall and
  Release_Thrall; the player gets the lesser power "Vampire's Thrall" (shout key)
  while a Vampire Lord - biting one's own thrall again sets them free. The vanilla
  feeding bite is played every time. MCM: Needs > Thralls.
- Thralls are kept in the save and told to the CHIM server on every load; AI agents
  who are thralls answer and act as thralls, the others know who obeys. A thrall who
  is not an AI agent still obeys: agents can undress / dress them (Undress and
  Get_Dressed now take a thrall's name) and bring them into scenes. A released
  thrall remembers having been one.
- Enemies can be enthralled, even mid-fight: the bite ends the fight for them and
  takes them out of the factions that made them hostile. New AI action Spare: keep
  someone alive (by name, or every hostile woman / man) - companions only beat
  them down. Thralls are protected the same way.
- Who can enthrall by default: the Vampire Lords of Dawnguard (now with the one left
  out before) and the whole Volkihar court - every noble of Castle Volkihar can bite
  and bind a thrall too (the court is not of the ancient blood of a Vampire Lord, so
  no Turn_Into_Vampire_Lord gift). The MCM list still adds anyone.
- Fix: when a bitten NPC turns into a vampire, the AI now hears of it (the event went
  out under a wrong name and was never commented on).
- Built on GitHub from source with the open-source papyrus-compiler: every release
  archive is made by the workflow from the tagged commit, no game files needed
  (tools/build_linux.sh, BUILDING.md).

1.1.0
- New: the vampire bite passes on Sanguinare Vampiris, the game's own disease
  (MCM toggle, chance per bite, lowered by disease resistance). The player turns
  the vanilla way after 3 days unless cured; an NPC turns after the days set in
  the MCM and is treated as a vampire by the mod from then on. The AI agents
  hear about it.
- New: Vampire Lords. Dawnguard's Vampire Lords, anyone in Vampire Lord form and
  NPCs listed in the MCM count as Vampire Lords; when asked, they can turn the
  player into a Vampire Lord with the new Turn_Into_Vampire_Lord action: the
  vanilla feeding bite, played to its end, then Dawnguard's own steps (vampire
  change, Vampire Lord power and perk tree; can be switched off in the MCM).
- New: solo scenes. An agent can pleasure themselves while the others watch
  (Start_Intimate_Scene with the kind "masturbation" and no partner), uses the
  installed solo animations.
- Changed: Feed_On_Blood and Turn_Into_Vampire_Lord wait until the agent has
  finished the line announcing the bite.
- Fixed: only the first bridge action after starting the game worked; every
  later one silently never reached the game (CHIM's command queue crashed on it).
  The bridge now has the direct entry point CHIM looks for first (the script COB
  with DispatchExternalCommand, the way other CHIM plugins receive their
  commands), instead of relying on CHIM's fallback mod event; results are
  reported the way CHIM's own actions report theirs (logMessageForActor).
- Fixed: replies got slow in places full of NPC scenes from other mods (an inn with
  OStim NPCs): every scene that ended nearby asked the AI for a comment, up to eight
  at once, and those requests held up the answers to the player for a minute. The
  player's own scene is still always talked about; a companion's at most every 30 s,
  anyone else's every 2 minutes.
- Fixed: "who climaxed" after a scene was read from the location text ("The Bee
  and Barb") instead of the names, so the agents were told the wrong people came.
- Fixed: after restarting the game, a reaction or an action identical to one from
  the previous session could be held back (timers in the save were ahead of the
  new session's clock).
- Fixed: after an OStim scene started by another mod (OStim NPCs and the like)
  with an AI companion in it, every bridge action of that companion stopped
  working until the game was restarted. The bridge cleared CHIM's "animation
  busy" flag on everyone in any finished scene, which breaks CHIM's command queue
  for an agent it had not marked busy; now it only clears the flag on actors it
  marked itself.
- Fixed: Feed_On_Blood did nothing (and Undress / Get_Dressed could fail) when the
  AI left the target empty (feeding on the player, undressing oneself). CHIM's
  command queue crashed on the empty last field before the game received the
  command; empty fields are now always sent as "-".

1.0.0
- First public release.
- AI actions: Start / Change / Stop intimate scene (partners, groups, kind of
  scene), Undress / Get_Dressed, Feed_On_Blood for vampires.
- Event reactions: cities, shops, own home, waking up, dragon souls, undressing,
  scenes nearby, talking after a scene.
- Needs: hunger for sex, blood thirst, drinking, arousal, jealousy.
- CHIM fixes: speech only, nobody talks after a long session.
- MCM through MCM Helper; settings are sent to the CHIM server automatically.
- MCM translated into 12 languages, following the game language (English otherwise).
- Server plugin installs and updates its CHIM actions by itself.
